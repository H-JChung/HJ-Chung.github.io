On the 2026–27 academic job market

I am a sociologist and social demographer studying stratification and inequality, with a focus on the educational experiences of youth and young adults. I am a Postdoctoral Research Associate at Princeton University, where I received my Ph.D. in Sociology and Social Policy and completed certificates in Population Studies and in Statistics and Machine Learning.

My research develops new tools to make visible the growing heterogeneity within higher education, and examines how institutional selectivity and field of study shape the labor market and family outcomes of the college-educated. I also develop causal inference methods to trace how inequalities of race, gender, and class emerge — both in the K–12 decisions that lead students toward higher education, and in the postsecondary experiences they go on to receive.
